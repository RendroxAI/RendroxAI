01 — Financial Operations Automation

Modern Trade Receipts Automation

An autonomous accounts-receivable clearing system that ingests multi-format payment advices from major retail partners, reconciles every figure against the enterprise ledger, and posts verified transactions — fully unattended.

450+Hrs Saved / Mo
$120kAnnual Savings
100%Unattended
⚠️

The Challenge

  • Major retail partners send payment advices in vastly different PDF and Excel formats with no standardization.
  • Finance teams spent 4+ hours daily manually matching invoices, calculating TDS deductions, price variances, and posting to the SAP ERP ledger.
  • Month-end volume spikes caused overtime backlogs. Quality depended entirely on who was processing the files.
  • Missed deductions and wrong adjustments quietly eroded margins, creating customer disputes and reconciliation delays.
✅

Our Solution

  • Deployed an unattended automation bot with 30+ orchestrated sub-automations running on an enterprise RPA platform.
  • Built dedicated layout extractors for 6+ vendor formats, folding every layout into a single unified data schema.
  • Automated reconciliation engine that computes TDS, rate differences, short payments, and round-offs before posting.
  • All unmatched items are quarantined to an exception queue — never mis-posted. Full audit trail on every transaction.

📋 End-to-End Workflow

01

Email Ingestion

Reads customer mails from a dedicated mailbox, saves attachments, and routes them to processing queues.

02

Advice Parsing

Customer-specific extractors parse each PDF/Excel format into a normalized invoice-line data model.

03

Ledger Reconciliation

Cross-checks advice against open items and the finance credit statement, line by line.

04

Difference Calculation

Computes TDS, rate differences, short payments, excess, and paisa-level round-offs.

05

ERP Posting & Report

Posts the cleared receipt document in SAP via F-28 and emails a rich summary with document numbers.

04 — SAP BPA Payment Advice Automation

MT Receipt — Payment Advice Processing Bot

An autonomous Payment Advice (PA) processing bot for Parle Products Pvt. Ltd., built on SAP Build Process Automation (BPA) within SAP BTP. Every hour it detects PA emails, downloads PDF/Excel attachments, extracts the data, and posts it straight into SAP — fully unattended.

1 hrAuto-Cycle
100%Unattended
PDFExcel
⚠️

The Challenge

  • Payment Advice emails arrived constantly in the "To Be Processed" folder and demanded manual download, review, and re-keying of data into SAP.
  • Attachments arrived in inconsistent PDF and Excel (.xlsx / .xls) formats, each needing bespoke handling.
  • Manual data entry introduced typos and mismatches, while there was no reliable audit trail for what had been posted.
  • Users had to manually track which emails were still pending, making delays and errors easy to miss.
✅

Our Solution

  • Deployed a scheduled MT Process Payment Advice Bot on SAP Build Process Automation (BPA), hosted on SAP BTP.
  • Configured a time-based trigger that runs automatically every 1 hour with zero manual intervention.
  • Automated mailbox scanning, attachment download (PDF/Excel), data extraction, and posting directly into SAP.
  • Processed emails are moved to the "Processed" folder, giving a clear, visual audit trail of completed items.

📋 End-to-End Workflow

01

Scheduled Trigger

Time-based trigger fires in SAP BTP automatically once every hour, with no manual action required.

02

Access Mailbox

Bot logs into the designated mailbox and navigates to the "To Be Processed" folder.

03

Scan & Identify

Scans all emails to identify those carrying a Payment Advice attachment in PDF or Excel format.

04

Download Attachment

Downloads each matching PA file (PDF/Excel) to a temporary working location for processing.

05

Post to SAP

Reads the file, extracts the Payment Advice data, and posts it directly into SAP — no manual data entry.

06

Move to Processed

Moves the email to the "Processed" folder, confirming the advice has been fully handled.
