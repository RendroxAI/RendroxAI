Deep-dive into the engineering, architecture, and measurable impact of three enterprise automation projects delivered by Aetheris AI.

01
Financial Operations Automation

Modern Trade AR Cleardown Bot

An autonomous accounts-receivable clearing system that ingests multi-format payment advices, reconciles every figure against the enterprise ledger, and posts verified transactions — fully unattended.

The Challenge

  • Major retail partners send payment advices in vastly different PDF and Excel formats.
  • Finance teams spent 4+ hours daily manually matching invoices, calculating TDS deductions, price variances, and posting to the ERP ledger.
  • Month-end volume spikes caused overtime backlogs. Quality depended entirely on who was processing.
  • Missed deductions and wrong adjustments quietly eroded margins, creating customer disputes.

Our Solution

  • Deployed a modular bot with 30+ orchestrated sub-automations running on an enterprise RPA platform.
  • Built dedicated layout extractors for 6+ vendor formats, folding every layout into a single unified data schema.
  • Automated reconciliation engine that computes TDS, rate differences, short payments, and round-offs before posting.
  • All unmatched items are quarantined to an exception queue — never mis-posted. Full audit trail on every transaction.

End-to-End Workflow

01

Email Ingestion

Reads customer mails from a dedicated mailbox, saves attachments, routes to processing queues.

02

Advice Parsing

Customer-specific extractors parse each PDF/Excel format into a normalized invoice-line data model.

03

Ledger Reconciliation

Cross-checks advice against open items and the finance credit statement, line by line.

04

Difference Calculation

Computes TDS, rate differences, short payments, excess, and paisa-level round-offs.

05

ERP Posting & Report

Posts the cleared receipt document and emails a rich summary with document numbers.

02
Sales & Integration

E-Commerce to ERP Sales Order Bot

An intelligent order-to-cash robot that converts high-volume storefront checkouts into fully configured ERP sales orders — with stock checks, bundle pricing, and payment reconciliation built in.

The Challenge

  • Thousands of e-commerce orders per day needed manual re-keying into the ERP, creating a major bottleneck during sales peaks.
  • Errors in material codes, pricing, plant assignments, and shipping addresses led to delivery failures and costly refunds.
  • Bundles and kits required complex price distribution across child items and multi-location stock checks.
  • Payments, refunds, and goods receipts had to stay financially synchronized across both platforms.

Our Solution

  • Built three cooperating robotic projects: a main creation robot, a decisions engine for fulfilment rules, and a change robot for amendments.
  • API-driven architecture using 25+ custom integration endpoints — no slow UI screen-scraping.
  • Bundle intelligence that explodes kit products, distributes parent prices across children, and assigns storage locations per item.
  • Closed-loop order-to-cash: collects payments, issues refunds, posts goods receipts automatically.

End-to-End Workflow

01

Configuration & Delta Check

Loads settings and reads the tracker table — only orders changed since last run.

02

Order Export & Mapping

Pulls new orders from the staging database and converts JSON to a full ERP sales order payload.

03

Stock & Location Logic

Decision table + live stock check across multiple storage locations determines fulfilment.

04

Sales Order Creation

Posts the order via API with a duplicate-existence guard ensuring idempotency.

05

Bundle Pricing & Payment

Distributes price to child items, applies discounts, and reconciles payments and refunds.

03
Supply Chain & Compliance

Logistics & Backorder Dispatch Bot

An integrated automation suite handling multi-channel order extraction, return processing, backorder release, and license-to-operate verification across distributed supply chain systems.

The Challenge

  • Managing order packing, shipping labels, and dispatch logistics across multiple marketplace channels required switching between 5+ legacy systems.
  • Return orders required manual validation against invoice history spanning a 7-year window, causing severe processing delays.
  • Backorder release involved checking real-time stock availability for each material and creating fresh sales orders and delivery documents.
  • License-to-operate data maintenance required manual verification of shipping addresses and customer master records.

Our Solution

  • Built 8+ specialized sub-bots: order extraction, return processing, invoice recalculation, backorder release, sales order creation, delivery generation, license maintenance, and blocked order clearance.
  • Automated the complete marketplace order lifecycle: API token retrieval, pending order fetch, status update, label download, and Excel consolidation.
  • Return processing bot validates against historical invoices (7-year window), categorizes items as matched/unmatched/beyond-policy, and generates tab-delimited clearing files.
  • Backorder bot checks real-time material availability, creates fresh sales orders, transfers quantities, and generates delivery documents automatically.

End-to-End Workflow

01

Order Extraction

Authenticates with marketplace APIs, retrieves pending orders, updates status to packed, and downloads shipping labels.

02

Return Processing

Reads email attachments, validates mandatory fields, matches against 7-year invoice history, and categorizes return items.

03

Backorder Release

Downloads blocked order lists, checks material availability, and creates new sales orders for available stock.

04

Delivery & Compliance

Generates outbound delivery documents and validates customer license-to-operate records.

05

Reporting & Notification

Compiles job summary reports with success/exception counts and emails results to operations teams.
